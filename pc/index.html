United States Army History

In a 1998 speech, the Army Chief of Staff, General Dennis Reimer, spoke of two armies: the "Functional Army" and the "Operational Army."
The Functional Army acquires, trains, distributes, educates, deploys, sustains and retires military assets. The Operational Army uses those assets to combat an enemy.
In his speech, Reimer, referring to the Functional Army, said, "This is the 'Other Army'
whose story is seldom told in song or rhyme but without whose efforts there would be no Operational Army to be the sword and shield of the nation."
The Functional Army to which General Reimer referred is the subject of this Podcast.
