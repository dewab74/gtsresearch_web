United States Army History

In a 1998 speech, Army Chief of Staff General Dennis Reimer spoke of two armies: the Functional Army and the Operational Army. The Functional Army acquires, trains, distributes, educates, deploys, sustains, and retires military assets. The Operational Army uses those assets to combat an enemy.

Reimer called the Functional Army "the Other Army", whose story is seldom told but without whose efforts there would be no Operational Army to be the sword and shield of the nation. The Functional Army is the subject of this podcast.
